About EVE

Where good taste meets great moments.

EVE is where Mumbai comes to slow down and stay a little longer. Long lunches turn into golden-hour cocktails, and dinners turn into nights you'll talk about for weeks.

Across Santacruz, Worli and Powai, each EVE has its own character and the same promise: beautiful food, handcrafted drinks and a room that feels like an occasion.
