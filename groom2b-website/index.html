Our Story

Every wedding has many chapters, and the groom deserves to look his best in every one. At Groom2B we dress you for each of them, from the first ritual to the last dance.

From our home in Jubilee Hills, opposite the Peddammathalli temple, we style grooms for every celebration. We bring together traditional South Indian silks, regal North Indian silhouettes and modern evening wear under one roof.

Whether you are getting married in Hyderabad or flying in from the USA, we help you put together a wardrobe that feels like you and suits your family's traditions.Across India & USA
